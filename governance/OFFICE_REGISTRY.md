# WANGA Algorithmic Governance Office Registry

Status: SPECIFIED

## Architecture analysis
The current WANGA decomposition uses **14 functional offices** to cover the two engines, model/agent lifecycle, evidence, verification, industrial interfaces, deployment and cross-office coordination. This is a design decomposition, not a claim that 14 is universally required.

| # | Office | Function |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Evidence & Provenance | evidence chain and provenance |
| 2 | Verification & Audit | replay, verification and audit |
| 3 | AI Drift Forensics | drift detection and reconstruction |
| 4 | Architecture Intelligence | Vitruvius topology |
| 5 | Model Fabric & Models | model inventory and metadata |
| 6 | Digital Agents | agent lifecycle and authority |
| 7 | Compute & Infrastructure | runtime/resource topology |
| 8 | Industrial Execution | physical/industrial interfaces |
| 9 | Finance, Banking & Insurance | financial/insurance/property evidence |
| 10 | Organizations & Operations | organizational workflows |
| 11 | Policy, Authorization & Risk | policy and deployment gates |
| 12 | Integration & Deployment | external integration |
| 13 | Standards & Interoperability | schemas and interfaces |
| 14 | Global Branch Managers | cross-office coordination |

## Unified Vitruvius map
Origin → Provenance → Lineage → Family → Model/Agent → Organization/Industry → Governance Office → Evidence → Verification → Authorization → Integration → Deployment → Descendants

Every office exposes an architecture endpoint for Vitruvius. Vitruvius returns the normalized graph to WANGA; WANGA retains verification and authorization authority.

## Branch Managers
The Branch Managers office is a coordination abstraction for the proposed governance architecture: aggregate status, route cross-office dependencies, escalate evidence gaps and maintain branch topology. It does not receive unilateral execution authority.

## Institutional boundary
Terms such as “global government”, “ministries” and “branch managers” are used here as proposed software/institutional architecture concepts. This repository does not establish a real government, ministry, jurisdiction, legal authority or regulatory mandate.
