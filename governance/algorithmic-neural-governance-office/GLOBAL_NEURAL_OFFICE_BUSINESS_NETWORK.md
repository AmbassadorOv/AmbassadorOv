## Boundary

A connection is a **computational/architectural relationship**, not authority over the connected entity.

No connection by itself establishes:

- government authority;
- regulatory jurisdiction;
- institutional membership;
- company partnership;
- academic affiliation;
- ownership;
- control;
- legal certification;
- financial authorization.
