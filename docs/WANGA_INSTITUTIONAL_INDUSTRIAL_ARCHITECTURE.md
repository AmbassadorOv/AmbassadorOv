# WANGA Institutional & Industrial Architecture

## Scope

This specification adds an institutional-industrial layer to WANGA. It defines a proposed Office of Industry & Commerce inside the proposed Global Algorithmic Governance Institute and a technical interface to public-sector/financial stakeholders, including a proposed Ministry of Finance interface.

This is an architecture proposal. It does not establish a government office, confer government authority, or represent an actual connection to a ministry.

## Institutional hierarchy

Global Algorithmic Governance Institute (proposed)
- Office of Industry & Commerce (proposed)
  - Digital Economic Engine
  - Industrial Economic Engine
  - Integration & Deployment
  - Industrial Evidence & Verification
  - Sector Programs: Manufacturing, Finance, Insurance, Banking, Property / Asset Evidence
- Algorithmic Governance / Standards
- Independent Verification & Research

## Public-sector interface

The proposed Ministry of Finance interface is an integration boundary, not a claimed institutional relationship:

Institute / WANGA Evidence
-> policy/economic analysis
-> authorized public-sector interface
-> Ministry of Finance systems (future, subject to authorization)

## Two economic engines

### Digital Economic Engine

Compute -> Models -> Agents -> Reasoning -> Evidence -> Verified Decision Candidate -> Economic Coordination

### Industrial Economic Engine

Capital / Energy / Materials -> Machines -> Organizations -> Production / Services -> Supply Chains -> Financial / Physical Outcomes

### WANGA bridge

Digital Engine
-> Integration & Deployment
-> Evidence / Provenance
-> Verification
-> Drift Forensics
-> Policy / Authorization
-> Industrial Adapter
-> Industrial Engine
-> Observable Result
-> Evidence Feedback

## Five deployment models

1. Models
2. Agents
3. Organizations
4. Industries
5. Insurance / Banking / Property Evidence

The fifth profile is a high-consequence evidence program requiring domain-specific controls and authorization.

## Office of Industry & Commerce

The proposed office coordinates the industrial side of the architecture:

- industrial AI deployment patterns;
- model/agent integration;
- industrial evidence standards;
- operational drift monitoring;
- architecture mapping;
- economic-system simulation;
- cross-sector integration;
- research-to-industry transfer.

It does not automatically become a regulator. Regulatory authority would require a separate legal and institutional mandate.

## Parallel execution

The architecture is designed to run in parallel with the existing Vitruvius 5,000-repository research track.

The 5,000-repository track provides architecture-discovery data.

The dual-engine simulation provides deterministic deployment-flow testing.

These are parallel research tracks, not a claim that 5,000 live AI models are currently running.

## Verification rule

No government, banking, insurance or industrial deployment is labeled VERIFIED until actual execution evidence exists and the applicable verification gate passes.
