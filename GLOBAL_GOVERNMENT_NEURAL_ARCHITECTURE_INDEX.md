`Neural Families → Thought / Capability Families → Models → Agents → Evidence → Verification → Drift → Vitruvius → Governance Offices → Business / Industry → WANGA Main Bridge`

## Global government / institutional corpus

The corpus is intended to classify public-sector and institutional architecture as externally sourced architecture nodes:

`External Government / Institution → Public Architecture Evidence → Family Classification → Vitruvius Mapping → Evidence / Provenance → Verification → Candidate WANGA Integration`

The objective is architecture understanding and provenance-traceable mapping, not representation of government organizations.
