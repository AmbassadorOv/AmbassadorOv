## Six directional positions

1. רום / מעלה — above
2. תחת / מטה — below
3. מזרח — east / before
4. מערב — west / behind
5. דרום — south / right
6. צפון — north / left

## Structural property

The text itself links the sealing operation to שש קצוות and then names six directional positions.

This gives a source-explicit sixfold directional/boundary structure.

## Important textual distinction

The six seals here are not a six-part taxonomy of names.
