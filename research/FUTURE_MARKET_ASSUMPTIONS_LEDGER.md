# Future Market Assumptions Ledger

This ledger tracks external evidence and extracted working assumptions for the five-year agentic-market scenario and the Continuous Logic Mine research program.

## Working scenario

**S5:** by approximately 2031, a large share of economically valuable knowledge-work execution is performed through autonomous or semi-autonomous AI systems.

S5 is a planning assumption, not a verified forecast.

## Collect each cycle

- organizations already building agent registries, skills, tools, autonomous research systems, or capability marketplaces;
- products that treat capabilities as reusable, discoverable, versioned assets;
- papers and standards describing capability discovery, routing, composition, adaptation, evaluation, provenance, and governance;
- evidence of autonomous capability creation or repair;
- gaps that imply a need for deeper logic architecture or future-ready capabilities.

## Assumption record

For each new observation record:

**DATE | SOURCE | OBSERVATION | EXTRACTED ASSUMPTION | STATUS | IMPLICATION | GAP | POSSIBLE IMPROVEMENT | EVIDENCE**

Never promote an assumption to a fact without supporting evidence.

## Current research signals

Google Cloud Agent Registry and AWS Agent Registry demonstrate that agents, tools, skills, MCP servers, discovery, semantic search, versioning, and governance are becoming infrastructure. 2026 research likewise describes agentic skills as reusable procedural artifacts with discovery, routing, composition, adaptation, evaluation, and security lifecycle.

These observations support the existence of an emerging capability layer. They do not prove the full Logic Mine thesis.

## Core question

Who is already operating from the S5 premise, what assumptions are embedded in their systems, what capabilities are becoming infrastructure, and what remains missing for a continuous Logic Mine that generates future-ready logic capabilities?

## Improvement loop

OBSERVATION → ASSUMPTION → EVIDENCE → GAP → ARCHITECTURE CHANGE → TEST → NEW OBSERVATION
