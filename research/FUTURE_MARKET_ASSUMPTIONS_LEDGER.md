# Future Market Assumptions Ledger

This ledger tracks external evidence and extracted working assumptions for the five-year agentic-market scenario and the Continuous Logic Mine research program.

## Working scenario

**S5:** by approximately 2031, a large share of economically valuable knowledge-work execution is performed through autonomous or semi-autonomous AI systems.

S5 is a planning assumption, not a verified forecast.

## Collect each cycle

- organizations already building agent registries, skills, tools, autonomous research systems, or capability marketplaces;
- products that treat capabilities as reusable, discoverable, versioned assets;
- papers and standards describing capability discovery, routing, composition, adaptation, evaluation, provenance, and governance;
- evidence of autonomous capability creation or repair;
- gaps that imply a need for deeper logic architecture or future-ready capabilities.

## Assumption record

For each new observation record:

**DATE | SOURCE | OBSERVATION | EXTRACTED ASSUMPTION | STATUS | IMPLICATION | GAP | POSSIBLE IMPROVEMENT | EVIDENCE**

Never promote an assumption to a fact without supporting evidence.

## Current research signals

Google Cloud Agent Registry and AWS Agent Registry demonstrate that agents, tools, skills, MCP servers, discovery, semantic search, versioning, and governance are becoming infrastructure. 2026 research likewise describes agentic skills as reusable procedural artifacts with discovery, routing, composition, adaptation, evaluation, and security lifecycle.

These observations support the existence of an emerging capability layer. They do not prove the full Logic Mine thesis.

## Core question

Who is already operating from the S5 premise, what assumptions are embedded in their systems, what capabilities are becoming infrastructure, and what remains missing for a continuous Logic Mine that generates future-ready logic capabilities?

## 2026-10-08 — Consolidated field and strategy conclusions

### Future as an observation field

The program does not claim to invent or control future outcomes. Future conditions generate meanings, requirements, constraints, and engineering problems that cannot be fully specified in advance. Present actions can influence conditions without determining the resulting meanings.

### Future-facing, not future-dependent

Build what is useful now while preparing for capabilities that may become necessary as autonomous systems become more complex. If market direction changes, update the relevant configuration or logic layer rather than treating the entire research program as invalid.

### Logic Mine asset

The asset is not limited to historical extraction. It includes logic identification, reconstruction, relations, derivations, boundaries, configuration, drift forensics, verification, and construction of more advanced logical structures.

SOURCE → EXTRACTION → RECONSTRUCTION → LOGIC IDENTIFICATION → RELATIONS / DERIVATIONS → BOUNDARIES → CONFIGURATION → DRIFT FORENSICS → VERIFICATION → ADVANCED LOGIC STRUCTURE

### Prospective engineering field

The working hypothesis is that familiar engineering capabilities—abstraction, decomposition, design, construction, testing, debugging, verification, and optimization—may increasingly be applied to logical structures and reasoning architectures as engineering objects. This is a research hypothesis, not a claim that a mature discipline already exists under this name.

### Source-selection rule

Do not infer extractable logic merely from the importance, meaning, literary value, or formal appearance of a source. Extractable logic requires a demonstrated continuous logical structure. Sources are evaluated individually.

### Evidence and market discipline

Keep observation, assumption, evidence, inference, and hypothesis distinct. A failed search is NOT FOUND IN SEARCH, not proof of non-existence. Market forecasts do not establish WANGA value. Market direction and market speed are separate variables.

### Operational loop

OBSERVATION → ASSUMPTION → EVIDENCE → GAP → ARCHITECTURE CHANGE → TEST → NEW OBSERVATION

The objective is cumulative capability: each cycle adds evidence, tested structures, configuration knowledge, and better identification of the next relevant logic layer.
