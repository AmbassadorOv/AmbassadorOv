# Emerging Agent Logic Market — Research Index

**Status: RESEARCH / NOT_YET_VERIFIED**

This index tracks evidence for the forward-looking thesis that AI agent ecosystems are developing a capability layer in which reusable, discoverable, verifiable, and evolving capabilities become infrastructure.

## Evidence to monitor

- Google Agentic Resource Discovery (ARD): capability publishing, discovery, and verification across the web.
  https://developers.googleblog.com/announcing-the-agentic-resource-discovery-specification/

- Google Cloud Agent Registry: agents, MCP servers, standalone skills, endpoints, publishers, and skill revisions; semantic capability search.
  https://docs.cloud.google.com/agent-registry/overview
  https://docs.cloud.google.com/agent-registry/search-agents-and-tools

- Google Research survey on Agent Skills: reusable/executable/portable capabilities and lifecycle from discovery through evaluation and governance.
  https://research.google/pubs/a-survey-on-agent-skills-externalized-procedural-knowledge-in-language-models/

- Microsoft Research SkillWiki: continuous evolution of agent skills with provenance-aware knowledge ingestion, governance, and execution feedback.
  https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/skillwiki-a-living-knowledge-infrastructure-for-agent-skills/

- Internet of Agents capability-discovery research: machine-interpretable capability publication, task-driven discovery, ranking, composition, and continual discovery.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19113

## Interpretation boundary

The evidence supports the existence of an emerging capability/discovery layer around agents.

It does not prove that a Continuous Logic Mine is already a market category, nor that WANGA-LAB owns a unique position in it.

The research hypothesis remains:

**continuous logic research → new logic architectures → future-ready capability products → deployment → evidence → next research seam**
