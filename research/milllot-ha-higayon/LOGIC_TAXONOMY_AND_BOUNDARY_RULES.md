# Millot HaHigayon — Logic Taxonomy and Boundary-Controlled Inquiry

## Scope

This file records the logical taxonomy supplied for שער י' and turns it into an investigation-control schema. It does not replace the primary text.

## 1. Hierarchical genus/species structure

### Superior genus

"והסוג אשר אין למעלה ממנו סוג אחר, נקראהו — הסוג העליון"

The superior genus is the highest genus in the hierarchy.

### Intermediate genera/species

"כל אחת מהן היא מין לגבי מה שלמעלה ממנה, וסוג לגבי מה שתחתיה"

An intermediate node is simultaneously a species relative to what is above and a genus relative to what is below. This is a relational role, not an absolute label.

### Last species

"והמין אשר אין תחתיו מין אחר אלא אישים בלבד, נקראהו — המין האחרון. כגון אדם"

The last species is the terminal species immediately above individuals.

### Individual

"וכל דבר מוגדר שאינו מתחלק לריבוי לפי המהות, כגון ראובן או שמעון, נקראהו — איש"

An individual is not subdivided into multiple individuals by essence in this scheme.

## 2. Five predicables

### Genus

"כל כלל המאחד כמה מינים השונים במהותם"

A common class unifying different species essentially.

### Species

"כל סוג המאחד אישים השווים במהותם העצמית"

A class unifying individuals sharing the same essential nature.

### Differentia

"והעניין העצמי המבדיל מין אחד משאר המינים אשר עמו בסוג"

The essential feature distinguishing one species from other species within the same genus.

### Proprium

"וכל עניין שאינו מעמיד את המהות, אך הוא נמצא לכל אישי המין ההוא בלבד ואינו נמצא לזולתו"

A universal characteristic of the species that does not constitute its essence.

### Accident

"וכל עניין שאינו מעמיד את המהות, והוא נמצא למין ההוא ולזולתו"

A non-essential characteristic that may be shared beyond the species.

Accident subtypes:
- inseparable accident — cannot be separated from its subject;
- separable accident — can be separated from its subject.

## 3. Ten categories

The supplied material identifies ten highest predicates/categories:
- substance;
- quantity;
- quality;
- relation;
- where/place;
- when/time;
- position/state of arrangement;
- possession/having;
- action;
- passion/being-acted-upon.

They function as an ontological classification layer and must not be collapsed into the five predicables.

## 4. Hadd versus rasm

### Hadd

"המאמר המורה על מהות הדבר, המורכב מסוגו הקרוב והבדלו המעמיד"

A definition indicating the essence through proximate genus and essential differentia.

### Rasm

"והמאמר המורכב מסוג הדבר וסגולתו או מקריו הקימים"

A descriptive formula using genus plus proprium or relevant stable accidents.

Boundary rule:

A rasm is not promoted to a hadd merely because it is informative.

## 5. Boundary-controlled inquiry

For an object x:

1. determine its ontological category;
2. determine its predicational role;
3. identify the nearest legitimate question class;
4. investigate only relations admissible at that level;
5. stop when the category has been exhausted;
6. if a new question belongs to a different category, reclassify instead of extending the old inquiry.

Formal control rule:

QuestionAllowed(x,q) := q belongs to the admissible relation-space of x's current classification.

A question outside that space is not automatically deeper.

## 6. Main research error

Over-analysis can destroy the object of investigation by forcing a category to answer a question belonging to another category.

Examples of category drift:
- asking an accident to supply the essence of its subject;
- treating a descriptive rasm as a formal hadd;
- treating an intermediate genus as if it were an ultimate genus;
- treating individual variation as if it changed species essence;
- introducing language-specific distinctions at a level that is explicitly common across languages.

## 7. Research principle

Investigate where variation begins; stop where the category is exhausted.

The common level is still operationally necessary for reference and domain identification. Comparative investigation should move to the first point at which the relevant difference actually appears.
