## Evidence labels

Use BUILT / SPECIFIED / PROTOTYPED / TESTED / VERIFIED / PLANNED / HYPOTHETICAL precisely. A mention in a file or repository list is not evidence that a token, exchange, wallet, smart contract, compliance process, or financial product exists.

## Research rules

- Preserve source URL, publisher, jurisdiction, publication/update date, access date, relevant passage, and interpretation.
- Separate primary legal/regulatory sources from commentary and market opinions.
- Record uncertainty and unresolved questions explicitly.
- Do not present a token price increase, investor return, or regulatory outcome as guaranteed.
- Do not publish personal legal conclusions as a substitute for qualified legal review.
- Before presenting a token or token-like unit as an actual product, document its purpose, rights, transferability, supply rules, governance, risk model, and legal review status.
