# Crypto Regulation & Compliance Research

**Status:** PLANNED — research index only. This directory is not a legal opinion, compliance certification, or authorization to issue or market a digital asset.

## Research modules

- **REG-01 — Jurisdiction and activity map:** Identify where the project, operators, users, and services are located and what activity is actually proposed.
- **REG-02 — Asset classification:** Research how relevant authorities distinguish payment, utility, investment/security, e-money, asset-referenced, and other token categories. Do not assume a label chosen by a project determines legal classification.
- **REG-03 — Issuance and public offers:** Identify potentially relevant disclosures, registration, authorization, exemptions, and restrictions; record primary sources and effective dates.
- **REG-04 — Service-provider obligations:** Research exchange, brokerage, custody, wallet, transfer, and other intermediary obligations only where those activities are in scope.
- **REG-05 — AML/CFT and sanctions:** Map applicable requirements and unresolved questions for the actual jurisdictions and activities.
- **REG-06 — Consumer and investor protection:** Disclosures, marketing claims, conflicts, complaints, safeguarding, and risk communication.
- **REG-07 — Tax and accounting:** Track questions requiring jurisdiction-specific professional review.
- **REG-08 — Privacy and data protection:** Identify personal-data flows, retention, access, and cross-border issues.
- **REG-09 — Governance and operational controls:** Roles, approvals, incident response, records, audit trails, and change control.
- **REG-10 — Regulatory change monitoring:** Maintain source, jurisdiction, effective date, change summary, impact, owner, and next review date.

## Source-register schema

For each item, record authority/publisher, jurisdiction, official source title and URL, publication and effective dates, date accessed, exact provision or section, activity and asset category to which it may apply, plain-language summary, open interpretation questions, status, reviewer, and next review date.

Suggested statuses: UNREVIEWED, RESEARCHED, COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED, REVIEWED.

## Initial primary-source targets

The jurisdiction list must be chosen based on the actual project scope. For any selected jurisdiction, prefer official legislation, regulator publications, and official guidance over blogs or vendor summaries. For EU-facing activity, determine whether MiCA and other EU financial, AML, privacy, and consumer rules are relevant to the actual asset and service; do not presume that any one regulation covers every obligation.

## Hard boundary

Do not claim that a proposed token is compliant, exempt, authorized, or outside securities/financial regulation without a documented, jurisdiction-specific assessment by qualified counsel. Do not treat a repository, smart contract, or white paper as a substitute for legal authorization.
