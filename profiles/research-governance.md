# Research, Governance & Scientific Infrastructure

## Executable research

The research program treats publications, code, data, workflows and verification records as potentially connected research artifacts.

A research artifact can move through:

**Source → Normalize → Execute → Observe → Preserve Evidence → Verify → Compose**

## Research composition

The Research Composition Fractal approach organizes work around recurring structures that can connect apparently different scientific domains.

Examples include:

- paper-to-agent research artifacts
- evaluation lifecycle re-entry under drift
- independent evaluation as an evidence layer
- physical-world evaluation of software agents
- inference-time reasoning and computational-cost accounting

## Architectural Capacity Preservation

A central research question is:

> Did the system preserve a person's ability to formulate and redesign the system itself?

The proposed two-track model distinguishes:

**Execution track:** Define → Delegate → Execute → Verify

**Architectural track:** Observe → Define → Compose → Model → Delegate → Verify → Recompose

This is a research hypothesis and design framework, not an empirical ranking of people.

## Candidate discovery

Candidate-discovery research focuses on public or consented evidence of:

- problem formation
- structural recomposition
- cross-domain composition
- assumption detection
- interface creation
- failure reconstruction
- experimental discrimination
- independent artifact production
- uncertainty preservation

No single scalar score is intended to determine a person's value or placement. Human review and provenance remain required.

## Governance interface

The broader infrastructure connects researchers, institutions, evidence artifacts, models, tests, infrastructure nodes, risk institutions, proposals and verification records without collapsing those distinct objects into one category.
