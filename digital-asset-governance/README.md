# Digital-Asset Governance & Risk

**Status:** PLANNED — research and control-design library.

## Research modules

- **GOV-01 — Roles and authority:** Who can propose, approve, execute, pause, or reverse material changes.
- **GOV-02 — Key and custody controls:** Key management, separation of duties, recovery, access logging, and incident procedures, where relevant.
- **GOV-03 — Smart-contract assurance:** Threat models, independent review, test coverage, upgrade controls, and evidence of deployed bytecode.
- **GOV-04 — Treasury controls:** Authorization thresholds, reconciliation, reporting, and conflict-of-interest controls.
- **GOV-05 — Market integrity:** Concentration, manipulation risks, insider access, conflicts, and transparent disclosures.
- **GOV-06 — Reward integrity:** Contribution provenance, duplicate detection, appeal process, and auditable reward decisions.
- **GOV-07 — Incident and continuity:** Response roles, communications, recovery criteria, and post-incident review.
- **GOV-08 — Audit evidence:** Immutable or tamper-evident logs where appropriate, reproducible checks, and clear verification boundaries.
- **GOV-09 — Governance changes:** Versioned proposals, impact assessment, approvals, and rollback plans.
- **GOV-10 — Risk register:** Risk statement, cause, impact, likelihood rationale, controls, owner, evidence, residual risk, and review date.

## Required separation

Keep these claims distinct: a design is documented; code exists; tests pass; a contract or service is deployed; deployment behavior is independently verified; legal or regulatory status has been reviewed. One status does not establish another.
